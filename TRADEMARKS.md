# Kurobara names and third-party marks

The [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE) covers the Kurobara software. As stated in
section 6 of that licence, it does not grant trademark rights beyond reasonable
and customary use in describing the origin of the work.

## Current status

This file is a non-normative notice, not an approved trademark policy. The
owner of the Kurobara name, the intended publication territories and services,
and any applicable registrations or common-law rights have not yet been
established in the public record. No logo or other visual brand asset is
included in the current candidate.

Exact EUIPO and USPTO searches dated 23 July 2026 found no exact `Kurobara`
result, but exact database searches are not trademark clearance. The scope and
limits of those searches are retained in the private publication review.

Until an authorised owner approves a final policy, this document does not claim
registration, grant permission on behalf of an unidentified mark owner, impose
additional restrictions on the Apache-2.0 software rights, or promise an
enforcement process.

## Factual references

Project and provider names may be used factually to describe software origin,
compatibility or an adapter. Such wording must be accurate and must not imply
sponsorship, certification, partnership or endorsement.

The names Hunter, Prospeo, Tavily, Exa, Apollo and People Data Labs identify
third-party services and may be trademarks or trade names of their respective
owners. Kurobara does not ship their logos and does not claim a trademark
licence or endorsement from them.

## Publication gate

Before public launch, an authorised person must identify the Kurobara name
owner or decide to rename the project, define the intended territories and
services, complete an appropriately scoped clearance, and approve any
normative trademark policy and durable contact route. The required decision is
tracked in the private publication review and must be read back from the final
candidate before launch.
