The bootstrap maintainer is the default decision owner, subject to
[GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), conflicts of interest, and required evidence.

## Responsibilities

- keep public contracts, architecture, and documentation coherent;
- review contribution scope, provenance, checks, and risk;
- record structural decisions and unresolved objections;
- maintain community and security policies;
- bind releases to identifiable source, checks, artifacts, and limits;
- use least privilege and recuse when a conflict prevents impartial judgment;
- plan continuity as the maintainer group grows.

## What this file means

This file declares project responsibility and decision scope. It does not by
itself grant repository administration, package publication, signing,
credential, or private-report access.

New maintainers are nominated through a governance change based on sustained
contribution, reliable review, project understanding, and community conduct.
The change must state scope, decision authority, required technical access, and
known conflicts.

A maintainer may step down at any time. Access must be removed separately and
verified on each relevant system. Emeritus status may recognize past work
without retaining authority or access.
